# 9. Conclusion and Future Work

## 9.1 Conclusion

The Gynecology Clinic Digitalization System was developed to support the daily workflow of a small outpatient gynecology clinic and reduce dependence on paper-based documentation. The system provides digital management for patient records, visits, prescriptions, attachments, appointments, billing, templates, and dashboard summaries.

The main contribution of the project is the design of a specialized EMR system with reusable medical intelligence through templates and workflow automation. It supports both pregnant and non-pregnant workflows, with the non-pregnant clinical workflow completed and validated first and the pregnancy module designed as an extension using the real clinic form. Overall, the project shows how small clinic digitalization can improve organization, reduce repeated clinical input, and support efficient patient record management without unnecessary enterprise-level complexity.

## 9.2 Future Work

Future work can focus on preparing the system for stronger professional use through improved backend security, complete role-based access control, audit logs, backup and recovery procedures, and wider testing with more clinic users and scenarios.

The system can also be expanded with cloud deployment, automated appointment reminders, analytics, multi-doctor access, and improved reporting tools. These additions would increase scalability while keeping the system aligned with the practical needs of small outpatient gynecology clinics.
